# ColonyMind artwork

These files are copied unchanged from the brand kit of
[Aevonix Research](https://aevonix.com/branding), the publisher of ColonyMind.
The logo is a symbol of three segments around a hexagonal core, a stencil
wordmark and, in the primary layout, the descriptor "Unified mind, memories
and autonomy for agents", set in capitals on two lines.

| Files | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `colonymind-primary-{dark,light}.{svg,png}` | Primary logo: symbol, wordmark and descriptor. The SVG canvas is 713.69 × 148; the PNGs are 1600 × 332 pixels. |
| `colonymind-compact-{dark,light}.svg` | Symbol and wordmark without the descriptor. The canvas is 605.99 × 80. |
| `colonymind-mark-{dark,light,black,white}.svg` | Standalone symbol in color or one ink. The canvas is 128 × 128. |
| `colonymind-avatar.png` | 512 × 512 pixels: the symbol on graphite. |

`dark` means light inks for a dark background; `light` supplies graphite
lettering and darker accent inks for a light background; `black` and `white`
are the symbol in one ink. The SVG files hold filled paths only: the lettering
is outlined and does not require a font. The PNGs of the primary logo have a
transparent background. The repository README shows the primary logo and
selects the dark or the light file by the reader's appearance.

## Color and layout

| Color | sRGB HEX | Where it is used in these files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite | `#151615` | Avatar background; lettering and core of the `light` files |
| Cream | `#F3E5CD` | Lettering and core of the `dark` files; core of the avatar |
| Amber | `#EEB07E` | Top segment of the `dark` files and the avatar |
| Coral | `#D99288` | Left segment of the `dark` files and the avatar |
| Teal | `#A9D5CE` | Right segment of the `dark` files and the avatar |
| Muted cream | `#C0B4A0` | Descriptor of the `dark` primary logo |
| Light-surface amber | `#854719` | Top segment of the `light` files |
| Light-surface coral | `#99483D` | Left segment of the `light` files |
| Light-surface teal | `#285F58` | Right segment of the `light` files |
| Light-surface muted ink | `#555449` | Descriptor of the `light` primary logo |

The names and values are those of the kit's `palette.json`. The one-ink
symbols are pure black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Use the
supplied light-background variants; their darker accent inks are already
applied. Keep the artwork's proportions and colors intact. Do not retype the
wordmark or add scanlines, glow or other effects through the logo.

Recommended minimum widths are **280 CSS pixels** for the primary logo,
**200 CSS pixels** for the compact logo and **24 CSS pixels** for the symbol.
Keep at least one quarter of the visible symbol's height clear around the
entire logo, including its descriptor. Measure that space from the visible
artwork, not from the canvas: in the primary PNGs the symbol is 163 pixels
high, so the clear space is 41 pixels at their full width, and the canvas
leaves less than that on the left, on the right and below (17, 26 and 28
pixels). The complete ColonyMind pack (the three layouts in dark, light, white
and black as SVG, PDF and PNG, the avatar and small icons), the palette files
and the brand guide are on the publisher's branding page linked at the top of
this page.
